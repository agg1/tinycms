However the studies were structured, the Telekommunikations-Informatik one in particular had combined several different branches of computer science, engineering, electronics, all at once, which i'll summarize with their German designation for sake of clarity:
0. Common basics of maths and physics
1. Technische Informatik (technical computer science, with digital circuit design, assembler programming)
2. Wirtschaftsinformatik (business computer science, with Java programming, Oracle databases, a little finance mathematics)
3. Theoretische Informatik (computer science theory, for encoding schemes, cryptography, artificial intelligence and a little related mathematics)
4. Angewandte Informatik Telekom (applied computer science, common focus on networking basics and specific focus on **both** internet **and** on telco backbone protocols such as ss7/isdn/etc)
5. Medieninformatik (media computing/creativity, web design, adobe premiere)
6. Elektrotechnik/Signalverarbeitung (notable amounts of electronics, signal processing, signals and systems, analog, and DSP)
